# puzPlayer Privacy Policy

_Effective November 24, 2026_

puzPlayer does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

puzPlayer keeps the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

- Puzzle files you open in the app (.puz, .ipuz and .jpz files)
- Your progress on each puzzle: letters entered, timer, checked and revealed squares, and similar
- Your folders, puzzle names and sorting choices
- Your settings, such as theme and grid colours

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not ask for any device permissions. It has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## Files you open

When you open or share a puzzle file into puzPlayer, the app reads only that file, which you chose, and saves a copy in its own storage. It does not look at any other files on your device.

## No tracking, ads or analytics

puzPlayer contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party code that collects data.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include puzPlayer's data, such as your puzzles and progress, in your device backup. Google manages that backup under your Google account and its privacy policy. You can turn it off in your device's settings. The app's developer never receives this data.

## Deleting your data

You can delete puzzles and folders inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Children

puzPlayer collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/dwhinsey/PuzzlePlayer/issues.
